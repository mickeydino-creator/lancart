How To Play

  • W / ↑ — Accelerate
  • S / ↓ — Brake / Reverse
  • A / ← — Steer Left
  • D / → — Steer Right
  • Space — Drift (hold + steer, release for boost)
  • Shift — Boost
  • P / Esc — Pause
